# `05` - Unknown status

| bmRequestType | bRequest | wValue | wIndex | wLength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `c0` | `05` | `0000` | `0000` | `0002` |

Reply, 2 bytes: `01 01`, every time it was read, in Normal and in Game mode. Its meaning is not known; it does **not** tell the work mode. The vendor software does not use it.
