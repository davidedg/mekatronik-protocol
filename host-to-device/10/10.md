# `10` - Write the macro index

| bmRequestType | bRequest | wValue | wIndex | wLength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `40` | `10` | `0000` | profile, `0001` to `0003` | `0069` |

Data: the 105-byte [macro index](../../formats/macro-index.md) of the profile: what each of the 20 macro keys does, and where its data is.

```
> 40 10 0000 0001 0069
  14 00 69 00 56 | 20 00 00 00 1b | 20 00 1b 00 1e | 11 00 39 00 02 | 00 00 00 00 00 ×9 | 20 00 3b 00 1b | 00 00 00 00 00 ×7
```

Write the [macro data](../12/12.md) that the index points to in the same profile write ([sequence](../../devices/meka-g-unit.md#write-a-profile)). Read back with [`11`](../11/11.md).
