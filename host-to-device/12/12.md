# `12` - Write the macro data

| bmRequestType | bRequest | wValue | wIndex | wLength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `40` | `12` | offset of the block | profile, `0001` to `0003` | block length, at most `1000` |

Data: a block of the [macro data](../../formats/macro-data.md) of the profile, whose total length is in bytes 3 to 4 of the [macro index](../10/10.md) written just before. **The data travels in blocks of at most 4096 bytes; wValue is the offset of the block in the data.**

```
27 bytes:
> 40 12 0000 0001 001b
  84 04 01 87 00 03 84 04 00 87 00 26 84 05 01 87 00 01 84 05 00 86 00 01 87 00 01

5724 bytes (0x165c):
> 40 12 0000 0002 1000   <bytes 0 to 4095>
> 40 12 1000 0002 065c   <bytes 4096 to 5723>

16326 bytes (0x3fc6):
> 40 12 0000 0002 1000   <bytes 0 to 4095>
> 40 12 1000 0002 1000   <bytes 4096 to 8191>
> 40 12 2000 0002 1000   <bytes 8192 to 12287>
> 40 12 3000 0002 0fc6   <bytes 12288 to 16325>
```

- **The Windows software sends nothing when the total length is 0.**
- **The keyboard completes the transfer only when it has stored the block**: the status stage of the 4096-byte block came 1.54 s after the setup, that of the 1628-byte block 0.83 s after. A host must allow a timeout of several seconds.
- After each block completed, the software waited a further 4.5 s (4096 bytes) and 2.5 s (1628 bytes) before the next request.
- The largest data written in these tests was 16326 bytes, in four blocks, to each of the three profiles: about 7 s for a profile with the waits of the [timing](../README.md#timing) section; the end of the data was stored and played. More was not tried.
