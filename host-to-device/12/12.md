# `12` - Write the macro data

| bmRequestType | bRequest | wValue | wIndex | wLength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `40` | `12` | `0000` | profile, `0001` to `0003` | total length of the data |

Data: the [macro data](../../formats/macro-data.md) of the profile, whose total length is in bytes 3 to 4 of the [macro index](../10/10.md) written just before.

```
> 40 12 0000 0001 001b
  84 04 01 87 00 03 84 04 00 87 00 26 84 05 01 87 00 01 84 05 00 86 00 01 87 00 01
```

- **The Windows software sends nothing when the total length is 0.**
- Every write seen was a single transfer with wValue `0000`, of at most 212 bytes. How the software sends data longer than that (several transfers, and what wValue then holds) has not been seen yet.
