# `16` - Write the macro-key map

| bmRequestType | bRequest | wValue | wIndex | wLength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `40` | `16` | `0000` | profile, `0001` to `0003` | `003f` |

Data: the 63-byte [macro-key map](../../formats/key-map.md) of the profile: the id and the repeat mode of each of the 20 macro keys.

```
> 40 16 0000 0001 003f
  14 00 01 01 01 02 01 03 02 04 03 05 01 06 01 07 00 08 00 09 00 0a 00 0b 00 d8 01 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 ×22
```

Read back with [`17`](../17/17.md) (profile 1 only).
