# `15` - Read the active profile

| bmRequestType | bRequest | wValue | wIndex | wLength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `c0` | `15` | `0000` | `0000` | `0001` |

Reply, 1 byte: the active profile, `01` to `03`. Seen: `01`.

The vendor software does not read it; it imposes its saved profile instead. Byte 7 of [`04`](../04/04.md) holds the same value.
