# `04` - Status

| bmRequestType | bRequest | wValue | wIndex | wLength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `c0` | `04` | `0000` | `0000` | `0008` |

Reply, 8 bytes:

```
offset  field
0       01 (always seen)
1       backlight level, 0 to 4: the backlight key's level, or the level of the last 31 to zone 0, 1 or 2 when on
2       01 (always seen)
3       03 (always seen)
4       light level of zone 0 (WASD)    0 to 3, 4 = pulse, 0 also when the zone is off
5       light level of zone 1 (arrows)
6       light level of zone 2 (keypad)
7       active profile, 1 to 3
```

Bytes 1 and 4 to 6 follow [`31`](../31/31.md) and the backlight key. They do not tell:

- whether a zone is off or at level 0 (both read `00`);
- the logo (`31` on zone 3 changes nothing here);
- the light shown after power-on: the keyboard then lights its default (zones 0 to 2 at level 3), but `04` still reads the values set before it lost power, until the first [`14`](../14/14.md), after which it read `03 03 03` (byte 1 `03`).

Seen:

```
01 00 01 03 00 00 00 01   first read
01 03 01 03 00 00 00 01
01 04 01 03 04 04 04 01   after the vendor software set the zones to pulse (31 wValue 0104)
01 00 01 03 00 00 00 02   after the profile 2 key and one press of the backlight key
01 03 01 03 03 03 03 03   after the zones were set to level 3, profile 3
01 00 01 03 00 00 00 01   zone 1 at level 0, zones 0 and 2 off
01 01 01 03 00 01 00 03   then the backlight key once (level 1: only zone 1 follows)
01 04 01 03 04 00 00 03   zone 0 pulse, zones 1 and 2 off; the same after power-on
01 03 01 03 03 03 03 01   after power-on and a 14
01 04 01 03 01 03 04 03   zone 0 level 1, zone 1 level 3, zone 2 pulse
01 03 01 03 04 01 03 03   zone 0 pulse, zone 1 level 1, zone 2 level 3
```

The vendor software does not use this request. Byte 7 gives the active profile like [`15`](../15/15.md).
