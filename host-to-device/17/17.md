# `17` - Read the macro-key map

| bmRequestType | bRequest | wValue | wIndex | wLength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `c0` | `17` | `0000` | profile, `0001` to `0003` | `003f` |

Reply: **40 bytes**, the 20 pairs `(id, repeat)` of the [macro-key map](../../formats/key-map.md) without the leading `14`.

**Only profile 1 reads back correctly.** For profiles 2 and 3 the firmware returns the area 16 and 32 bytes further on, which mixes the end of the previous table with other data:

```
profile 1:  00 01 01 00 02 00 03 00 04 00 05 00 06 00 07 00 08 00 09 00 0a 00 0b 00 00 00 ×8
profile 2:  08 00 09 00 0a 00 0b 00 00 00 ×8 cc 01 cd 01 ce 01 cf 01 d0 01 d1 01 d2 01 ...
profile 3:  00 00 ×4 cc 01 cd 01 ce 01 cf 01 d0 01 d1 01 d2 01 d3 01 d4 01 d5 01 d6 01 d7 01 d8 01 d9 01 da 01 ...
```

(read before profiles 2 and 3 were ever written). The vendor software does not read it. To know the repeat modes of profiles 2 and 3, keep them on the host.
